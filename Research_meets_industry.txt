RESEARCH MEETS INDUSTRY: NOTES
==============================

Topic:   Research meets industry
Course:  Cultural Data Analysis
Author:  Sönke Erdmann
Date:    26 November 2026

Notes on what changes when research on data and AI meets industry practice.
Each point comes with a short question to keep in mind when working with a
dataset or an AI system.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DATA AND RESPONSIBILITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Data minimisation for sensitive or human data
   Collect and keep only what the question needs, especially when the data
   is about people or is sensitive.

2. Compliance ≠ ethical AI usage
   Following the law (e.g. GDPR, the AI Act) is the minimum. Something can be
   compliant and still be unethical.

3. Human in / on the loop
   Does a person make or check each decision (in the loop), or oversee the
   system as a whole (on the loop)?

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AUTOMATION AND AI
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. How much automation is needed?
   Which steps actually benefit from being automated, and which should stay
   with people?

5. How fuzzy is the data? What's the benefit of AI?
   Is the data messy or ambiguous enough that AI adds something, or would a
   simpler method do the job?

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. What does the data tell? What does it hide?
   Interpretability of the data: what can be read from it, and what stays
   invisible?

7. Hardening of constructed categories
   Categories are made by people. Once they are built into data and systems,
   they start to look natural and fixed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHODS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. Stakeholder matrices, requirements engineering, human-centred design
   Methods for bringing people into the design: stakeholder matrices,
   requirements engineering, personae and scenarios.
